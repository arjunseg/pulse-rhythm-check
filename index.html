Pulse Rhythm Check

An educational tool that estimates how regular your pulse rhythm is, using your phone's rear camera and a fingertip.

Not a medical device. This app does not diagnose any condition. It has not been validated against ECG. If you have symptoms or concerns about your heart rhythm, contact a clinician.

How to measure

  1. Sit down and rest quietly for a minute.
  2. Lay the phone flat on a table, camera side up.
  3. Cover the main rear camera lens with the pad of your index fingertip and let your finger lie flat so the flash shines into it. Press lightly: pressing hard squeezes the blood out and the pulse disappears.
  4. Check the small round preview: it should glow an even red.
  5. Keep still for about 50 seconds (5 s to settle + 45 s recording).
